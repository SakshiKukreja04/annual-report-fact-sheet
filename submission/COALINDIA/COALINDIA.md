# COALINDIA FY25

## Company overview

Coal India Limited produces coal through its subsidiaries Mahanadi Coalfields Limited, Central Coalfields Limited, Northern Coalfields Limited, Western Coalfields Limited, South Eastern Coalfields Limited, Bharat Coking Coal Limited and Eastern Coalfields Limited and sells it to power and non-regulated sectors.

Coal India Limited’s seven mining subsidiaries operate a mix of underground, opencast and mixed mines across India, collectively producing 781.06 MT of coal in FY 2024-25. The consolidated off-take reached 762.98 MT, with the majority supplied to the power sector and a growing share to non-regulated consumers. Sales generated gross revenue of ₹1,91,357 crore, while the group recorded a profit before tax of ₹46,966.19 crore. The company’s operations are supported by extensive mine projects, mechanised loading systems and consumer-focused supply mechanisms.
